# Textora 1.4.0

Your writing preferences, a clearer workspace, and a way to recover useful results. New features stay off until you choose to use them.

## My Style
Choose how your writing should sound, then reuse that profile everywhere or in selected apps.
- Start in Settings > Writing > My Style. Edit Everyday, Work or Personal, then turn My Style on.
- Fix corrects mistakes with minimal changes. Formal keeps a professional tone. Your profile adds preferences to the action you choose.
- Example: Work can keep British spelling and product names while Formal makes a message more professional.

## Message
Preview dictated text locally, then optionally improve it before inserting.
- In Settings > Dictation > Output & Translation, choose Message. You can also switch while recording.
- With translation off, the local transcript becomes an editable draft without an AI request. Review it, then choose Insert or Copy.
- Choose Improve with AI to polish the transcript with your writing profile. Enabling translation also uses your AI provider. Audio stays on your Mac.

## Recent Results
Recover original text and finished results without repeating an AI request.
- In Settings > Recent Results, choose whether to save. History starts off.
- Search, copy or delete results from the Recent Results window, the result toolbar or Control-Option-Command-H.
- After enabling: 7 days and 200 results, adjustable in Settings. Text is encrypted locally; audio is never saved.

## Improvements
- Settings now has a sidebar and focused tabs for writing, dictation, shortcuts, permissions and updates.
- Update dialogs show the release’s changes before installation.

## Dictation improvements
- Press the shortcut once to record without holding keys. Stop or press it again to finish; hold-to-record remains available in Shortcuts.
- Choose Text or Message directly on the recording panel. Expand translation options to select From, Auto-detect and To before stopping.
- Recording controls remain stable while the audio meter updates. Using the controls or switching apps does not end recording.
- Improved focus resolution for editors such as Slack. When no field can be verified, keep the transcript or message draft available to copy without attempting an unverified paste.

## Local draft privacy
- Message no longer sends an automatic AI request when translation is off. Local previews work without an AI provider.
- Local draft and AI draft are clearly labelled. The selected writing profile applies only when AI processing is requested.
- Explicit AI improvement keeps the local transcript available if the provider fails, and late responses cannot replace a newer local draft.
